About this project

I built Cunto Tracker because the calorie apps I tried had no entries for the food I eat at home. A plate of bariis iskukaris or a morning of laxoox and shaah never showed up in search, so tracking meant guessing.

You create an account, fill in a short profile, and the app suggests a daily calorie goal. Then you log Somali dishes by serving and see your total for the day.
